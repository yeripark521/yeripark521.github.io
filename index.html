$ about

I'm an MSCS student in Computer Architecture, bringing 4.5 years of industry experience as an ML systems engineer at Hyundai Motor Company, where I optimize deep neural networks for real-time deployment on autonomous-driving hardware — spanning quantization, pruning, mixed-precision inference, and RTOS-based deployment frameworks across GPU, NPU, and embedded SoC platforms.

I hold an M.S. in Computer Science from the Georgia Institute of Technology and a B.S. in Electronic and Electrical Engineering from Ewha Womans University. I'm preparing to pursue doctoral research at the intersection of hardware architecture and efficient AI systems, with a particular interest in GPU microarchitecture and hardware-software co-design for next-generation ML acceleration.

Outside of work, I'm based in Atlanta, United States, where I'm continuing my graduate studies while staying close to the embedded and autonomous systems community.
